Privacy Policy for Smash It

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Smash It is a simple offline arcade game developed as an independent mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information We Collect

Smash It does not collect, request, or store personal information.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide information such as their name, email address, phone number, location, or other personal details.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Smash It may store your high score locally on your device so that your best score can be displayed when you return to the game.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

3. Internet and Network Access

Smash It is designed to work offline and does not require an internet connection to play.

The app does not intentionally transmit gameplay data or personal information to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Smash It does not use third-party advertising services, analytics services, social media SDKs, or other third-party services that collect personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

Smash It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Because the app does not collect personal information, users can play without providing personal details.

6. Data Security

Smash It does not collect or transmit personal information to external servers.

Any locally stored game information, such as the player's high score, remains on the user's device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Smash It changes or if necessary to reflect applicable requirements.

Any updated version will replace the previous version.
